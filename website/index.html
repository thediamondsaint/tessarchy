THE TESSARCHY NETINSTALLER

One small
download.

Download one ISO under 1 GiB. Connect to the internet and the installer fetches Tessa and Tessarchy during setup.

Download netinstall ↓Release notes and checksums ↗

The installer needs UEFI, an internet connection, and a 24 GiB or larger disk. It erases the selected disk after you confirm its name.

01 / BEFORE INSTALLING

Check the basics.

This alpha needs an x86_64 UEFI computer, a working internet connection, and a 24 GiB or larger target disk. Wi-Fi support includes common Intel, Atheros, Broadcom, MediaTek, and Realtek devices.

Read netinstall requirements ↗
02 / FIRST-TIME SETUP

Make Tessarchy yours.

Choose your username, device name, password, and time zone in the installer. On the first sign-in, Tessa helps you pick a theme and accent color. Change them again later in Settings.

See the install flow ↗
03 / THE DEVELOPMENT VM

Build and explore.

The separate Arch development image is a clean base for testing Tessarchy changes. Its release includes a checksum and detached signature.
