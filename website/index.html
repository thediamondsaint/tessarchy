UNDER THE SURFACE

THE DESKTOP IS CALLED TESSA

Not a skin.
A desktop in progress.

Tessa brings together a custom Quickshell interface and Hyprland on Arch Linux. The goal: one thoughtful environment that can change shape without losing its character.

A

Layouts that respond

Choose automatic sizing or set a phone, tablet, or desktop layout yourself.

B

Controls close by

Pull-down settings bring display, audio, navigation, and available radio controls within reach.

C

Apps with a place here

Launcher, files, settings, phone, messages, and other tools are being shaped around touch and keyboard use.

D

Made in the open

Source, package recipes, validation notes, and open work live alongside the project.

NO MYSTERY ABOUT THE MILESTONE

Early, open,
moving forward.

Tessarchy is an experimental operating system. We separate what boots today from what still needs hardware, testing, and time.
