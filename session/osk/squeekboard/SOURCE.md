# Keyboard layouts

US layouts adapted from Squeekboard v1.43.1, licensed GPL-3.0-or-later:
https://gitlab.gnome.org/World/Phosh/squeekboard/-/tree/v1.43.1/data/keyboards

Adds a latched Mod4 (Super) key to standard and terminal views. Terminal layouts retain Control/Alt with four full-height rows; Fn exposes navigation and F1–F12. The T-cube is the Super key. Tap Super, then the shortcut key; tap
Super again to cancel it. Other languages retain Squeekboard defaults.
